# CargoDB

## Project Vision

### The Idea

**CargoDB** is an international SaaS platform for companies that deliver goods.

We are not building yet another CRM.

Our goal is to create a free platform that genuinely helps small cargo companies get started—and gradually becomes their primary day-to-day tool.

---

## Strategy

### Community Edition

Any company can start using the platform for free.

They get everything they need for daily operations:

- Products
- Shipments
- Status tracking
- Finance
- Customer portals
- Multilingual interface

Our mission is to build a large international user community as quickly as possible.

### Enterprise Edition

As companies grow, they face challenges that the free tier cannot solve.

For that, we offer **Enterprise Edition**:

- Implementation
- On-premise deployment
- Custom development
- Integrations
- Ongoing support

### CargoChat

**CargoChat** is a separate product that connects to CargoDB.

It automates customer communication, document processing, and AI-powered service workflows.

---

## How We Plan to Make Money

**Community Edition stays free.**

We expect revenue from several sources.

### AI

Small companies rarely pay hundreds of dollars per month.

But **$20/month** for an AI assistant that actually saves time is a realistic price point.

If we reach:

| Companies | Monthly revenue |
|-----------|-----------------|
| 1,000 at $20/month | $20,000 |
| 5,000 at $20/month | $100,000 |

### Enterprise

As businesses grow, some inevitably outgrow Community Edition.

They will need:

- Dedicated servers
- Enhanced security
- Custom features
- Integrations
- CargoChat
- Technical support

These projects carry significantly higher budgets and become the main source of profit.

---

## Why This Could Work

Every month, new cargo companies appear around the world.

Almost all of them share the same reality:

- Excel
- WhatsApp
- Documents scattered across chats
- No proper tracking

They are not ready to buy expensive enterprise software.

We want to give them a modern tool for free, grow alongside them, and earn when their business itself starts needing deeper automation.
